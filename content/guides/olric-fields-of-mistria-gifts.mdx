# Olric Fields of Mistria Gifts: Loved and Liked

Olric Fields of Mistria gifts are mostly ores, gems, rocks, and mining-related items. Give him a Perfect Ore or Perfect Gem from the confirmed loved list for the strongest documented preference, or use ordinary ore, gems, stone, and selected food from the liked list when you do not have a perfect item. Avoid junk, bugs, weird gifts, and especially Rockbiter; the collected sources identify Rockbiter as his hated gift.

## Olric at a glance

The collected profile source describes Olric as a character who runs the Blacksmith with his brother March. It lists Spring 10 as his birthday and identifies Olin and Jade as his parents. These details give useful context for players planning routine gifts and a birthday visit.

The supplied secondary material calls Olric non-romanceable at the time it was written, but relationship and romance systems can change across updates. This article focuses on gifts rather than promising a current romance status. Any newer relationship availability not confirmed by a current official source is **To be confirmed**.

## Loved gifts

The project archive's Fandom extraction and Steam gift guide agree that Olric loves Perfect Copper Ore, Perfect Diamond, Perfect Emerald, Perfect Gold Ore, Perfect Iron Ore, Perfect Mistril Ore, Perfect Pink Diamond, Perfect Ruby, Perfect Sapphire, and Perfect Silver Ore. The common theme is clear: perfect-quality mined ore and gems are his confirmed favorites.

These are valuable materials, so a loved gift may compete with crafting, upgrades, or museum needs. The sources do not provide a friendship-point table for every gift tier, and they do not state how a perfect item is generated in the current version. Do not treat a loved gift as the only valid way to build friendship when a liked item is easier to spare.

### Perfect copper and ruby

The collected Destructoid guide says Perfect Copper Ore and Perfect Ruby can drop from their respective nodes on mine floors 1 through 20. This aligns them with the early mine tier. It does not establish a drop percentage, whether every node type can produce a perfect version, or the best route for farming them, so those details are **To be confirmed**.

If you are mining early floors anyway, keep an eye on these perfect finds rather than selling or using them immediately. A current inventory label should be treated as the authority for item quality. Do not infer that a normal copper ore or ruby becomes perfect through crafting; the collected sources describe perfect items as possible node drops.

### Perfect iron and sapphire

The same secondary guide places Perfect Iron Ore and Perfect Sapphire in the mine range between floors 21 and 40. These are the middle-depth loved options in the supplied collection. They can be practical gifts once that mine tier is open, but availability on a given day and exact node behavior are **To be confirmed**.

Normal Iron Ore and Sapphire are also on the liked list, so do not wait for perfection if you want a reliable gift from this tier. The distinction between liked and loved is important: both are documented preferences, but the archive does not give a precise numerical difference in friendship gain.

### Perfect silver and emerald

Perfect Silver Ore and Perfect Emerald are described as possible drops from floors 41 through 60 in the collected Destructoid material. They are later-mine options for Olric's loved list. The profile and Steam guide also include Perfect Diamond, Perfect Gold Ore, Perfect Mistril Ore, and Perfect Pink Diamond, but the supplied extraction does not give their individual location or acquisition instructions.

For those unspecified perfect items, the correct status is **To be confirmed**. A guide should not invent mine floors, vendors, recipes, or prices just because the item name fits a mining pattern. Use the loved list as confirmed preference data and seek a verified source before adding acquisition steps.

## Liked gifts

Olric's liked list is much broader. The sources agree on Copper Ore, Crystal, Diamond, Emerald, Gold Ore, Hard Boiled Egg, Iron Ore, Miner's Mushroom Stew, Mistril Ore, Obsidian, Pink Diamond, Rock with a Hole, Rockroot, Ruby, Sapphire, Silver Ore, Stone, Stone Horse, Rock Statue, Weightless Stone, and Rockroot. This gives players several options when they do not have a perfect ore or gem.

Some archive sources differ in formatting, but they consistently connect Olric with mining materials, geological objects, and a few food or artifact items. If a current in-game character page shows a changed preference, use that current source instead. This list is based on the archived materials and should be version-checked before a permanent data-table import.

### Everyday mining gifts

Copper Ore, Iron Ore, Silver Ore, Gold Ore, Mistril Ore, Crystal, Obsidian, Diamond, Emerald, Pink Diamond, Ruby, and Sapphire are named liked gifts. Normal ore and gems are generally easier to replace than their perfect counterparts, which makes them sensible choices if you are also progressing through the mine. The sources do not rank one liked ore above another.

The Destructoid extraction gives several location hints: Copper Ore and Ruby on floors 1 through 20, Iron Ore and Sapphire on floors 21 through 40, and Silver Ore, Crystal, and Emerald on floors 41 through 60. It also says Copper and Iron Ore can be bought from the Blacksmith after specified mine progression. Current prices, stock rules, and unlock conditions should be checked in-game because those details are version-sensitive.

### Food and crafted options

Hard Boiled Egg and Miner's Mushroom Stew are listed as liked gifts. The collected guide describes Hard Boiled Egg as made at a cooking station with one Chicken Egg, and Miner's Mushroom Stew as a level 2 cooking recipe using an Upper Mines Mushroom and a Wild Mushroom. These are useful alternatives if you prefer cooking or animal care to mining.

The archive does not establish where every recipe is acquired, whether a recipe can change between versions, or the friendship value of each dish. Treat the cooking directions as secondary-source guidance. If a recipe or ingredient differs in your build, mark the discrepancy and use the in-game recipe book as the primary reference.

### Rocks, artifacts, and forage

Stone, Rockroot, Rock with a Hole, Stone Horse, Rock Statue, and Weightless Stone appear in the liked lists. The gathered guide says Stone comes from mining rocks, Rockroot can be found in mine floors 21 through 40, Rock with a Hole can be fished after the Aquatic Antiquities perk, and Stone Horse can appear as an artifact in floors 41 through 60. These acquisition notes are not all repeated in the official excerpt, so they should remain attributable to the source rather than treated as immutable rules.

Rock Statue and Weightless Stone are confirmed as liked preferences in the archive, but no verified acquisition details were supplied for this project. Their location, value, and availability are **To be confirmed**. Leaving that gap visible is better than creating a false farming route.

## Birthday gifting

Olric's profile lists Spring 10 as his birthday. The collected Destructoid guide recommends giving a loved or liked gift on that day to boost hearts, and the TechRaptor excerpt says birthday gifts result in more friendship gain. This supports saving a documented favorite for Spring 10 when you can.

Neither excerpt in this project provides the exact bonus, whether it stacks with other events, or the time window for the birthday. Those values are **To be confirmed**. The practical answer is simply to check the calendar, bring a confirmed liked or loved item, and verify the date in your current save.

## Gifts to avoid

The Fandom extraction names Junk, Bugs, and Weird Gift as disliked categories, with Rockbiter as Olric's hated gift. The Steam gift guide gives the same conclusion and includes a unique negative reaction for Rockbiter. The collected Destructoid guide expands junk with examples such as Fiber, Hay, Cattail, Acorn, Pinecone, Sod, and Sap.

Do not give Rockbiter if your goal is friendship. Its location as a mine fish is not a reason to treat it as a mining-themed present; the sources specifically distinguish it as the item Olric hates. The exact numerical penalty or dialogue in the current version is **To be confirmed**.

## A practical gift plan

For an early mine route, ordinary Copper Ore or Ruby are confirmed liked options, while a Perfect Copper Ore or Perfect Ruby is a confirmed loved option if you find one. In middle and later mine tiers, normal Iron Ore, Sapphire, Silver Ore, Crystal, or Emerald can serve the same practical role. This avoids spending all of your rare perfect drops while still following Olric's documented preferences.

If you do not want to mine, Hard Boiled Egg and Miner's Mushroom Stew are documented liked alternatives. For any item not listed as loved, liked, disliked, or hated, the profile source says it is neutral. Check Olric's current in-game preference record after gifting, because it can be the best way to notice changes after an update.
