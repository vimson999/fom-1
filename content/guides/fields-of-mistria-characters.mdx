# Fields of Mistria Characters: Romance and Villagers

Fields of Mistria characters include romance candidates, townsfolk, vendors, and other figures tied to the town’s story. To build a relationship, speak with villagers and give gifts that match their preferences; the collected material says conversations and quest turn-ins share an interaction key, with a quest turn-in taking priority when both are available. The most dependable approach is to meet people naturally through daily play, use the journal to record preferences, and avoid guessing at a favorite gift.

## Who can you romance?

The collected villager list identifies twelve single characters: Adeline, Balor, Caldarus, Celine, Eiland, Hayden, Juniper, March, Reina, Ryis, Seridia, and Valen. The same source groups Adeline, Celine, Juniper, Reina, Seridia, and Valen as bachelorettes, and Balor, Caldarus, Eiland, Hayden, March, and Ryis as bachelors. The player can choose their own presentation; do not infer romance availability from a character’s appearance or from a fan video.

The supplied material describes romanceable villagers with a purple heart icon in dialogue, but visual indicators should be checked in the current build. Some characters, including Caldarus and Seridia, may be connected to later story progress in community sources. Their exact unlock requirements are **To be confirmed** unless you verify them with a current in-game save or official documentation.

## Meet the core residents of Mistria

Adeline is described in the collected sources as the local baron’s daughter who is helping run Mistria with her brother Eiland. Eiland works with archaeology and the museum, while Celine is associated with gardening near the edge of town. Hayden runs Sweetwater Farm west of the player’s farm, and March is the blacksmith who lives with his brother Olric.

Balor is presented as a merchant, Ryis as the carpenter, Juniper as the bathhouse owner who works with potions, and Valen as the town doctor. Reina works at the Sleeping Dragon Inn and is described as an aspiring cook. These role descriptions are useful for locating people and understanding the town, but they are not substitutes for following the map markers and schedules in your own save.

## Townsfolk, family groups, and vendors

The gathered villager list separates non-romanceable townsfolk from traveling and market vendors. Town residents named in that source include Dell, Dozy, Elsie, Errol, Hemlock, Henrietta, Holt, Josephine, Landen, Luc, Maple, Nora, Olric, and Terithia. The same material names Darcy, Louis, Merri, Stillwell, Taliferro, Vera, Wheedle, and Zorel as vendors.

Several relationships help make the town easier to remember. Hemlock and Josephine run the Sleeping Dragon Inn and are the parents of Reina, Maple, and Luc according to the collected character material. Holt and Nora run the General Store and are listed as Celine and Dell’s parents; March and Olric are brothers. These facts are included because they recur across the sources, but any schedule or shop inventory connected to them should be verified in-game.

## Talk, give gifts, and track preferences

The collected sources say that villagers have distinct gift tastes and that a player can speak to them or give them a gift to build hearts. A secondary guide lists many loved, liked, disliked, and hated items for individual characters. It also says that most characters dislike broad categories called Junk and Weird Gifts, but category labels and exceptions are **To be confirmed** against the current game before you use them as a universal rule.

The safest gift strategy is small and traceable. Give an item, watch the in-game reaction, and record the result in the journal rather than repeating an online list that may be outdated. The supplied beginner transcript specifically advises paying attention when characters mention preferences in dialogue and keeping liked or loved items in the journal as you discover them.

## Examples of supported gift information

The project’s collected GameSpot material lists coffee, lemon pie, and several flowers among Adeline’s loved gifts, while Celine’s list emphasizes flowers such as roses, crystal roses, and frost lilies. It lists gold ingots and several ores among March’s loved gifts, and animal products such as golden butter, golden cheese, and golden eggs among Hayden’s loved gifts. Use those as source-backed examples, not as a guarantee that every entry has remained unchanged after future updates.

For characters outside the romance pool, the same collected source includes individual preferences as well. It lists bugs for Luc, cooking and tea-related items for Josephine, and fish, bugs, or specific materials for many other villagers. Do not turn this page into a complete gift database until the names, categories, and responses have been checked against a reliable current source; unverified individual entries should remain **To be confirmed**.

## Dating after heart progress

The collected Villagers source states that, after reaching eight hearts with a single villager, Ari can confess and begin dating. It says Elsie visits the farm to explain dating, and that a player can invite a partner on one date per week on weekends. Because this is a community wiki source rather than an official rules page, the heart threshold, date frequency, and invitation timing should be treated as **To be confirmed** for a current-version walkthrough.

The same source identifies date inspiration items: the Inn Menu is said to unlock an Inn Meal Date, while a Picnic Set is said to unlock a Deep Woods Picnic Date. It also says a first date of a new type awards a photo card that can be placed in the home. These are useful leads for planning a romance page, but this article avoids inventing the exact item locations, rewards, or dating dialogue.

## Romance candidates at a glance

Adeline is associated with town restoration and administration; Eiland with archaeology; Celine with gardening; and Reina with the inn and cooking. Hayden is tied to farm life and animals, while March is tied to blacksmithing. Balor’s merchant role, Ryis’s carpentry, Juniper’s bathhouse and potion work, and Valen’s clinic give each candidate a clear place in the town’s daily map.

Caldarus and Seridia are also listed as single in the collected reference, but their presentation is more closely tied to story and mine discoveries. The exact point at which they become available for romance, their gift records, and their heart-event requirements are **To be confirmed** here. A good player guide separates the confirmed character roster from progression claims that need a current check.

## Use recurring social moments

A collected beginner transcript highlights Friday evenings at the Sleeping Dragon Inn as a time when townsfolk gather, after a letter from Reina introduces the event. This can make a social round more efficient than chasing people individually, and it can also be a place to turn in suitable requests. The exact start time and attendance pattern are **To be confirmed** in the current version.

The Saturday Market is another opportunity to meet vendors and see changing stock. Its vendor list and availability may change as the game develops, so treat every roster, item, and date as a version-specific detail. The practical lesson remains: use the map, recurring events, and the Request Board to encounter people in context instead of treating relationships as a checklist.

The project’s sources provide substantial community documentation, but not a single current official database covering every interaction.
