# Wedding Outfits Fields of Mistria: Options & Planning

Wedding outfits Fields of Mistria are part of the wedding-event cosmetics introduced with the 1.0 update. The official patch notes confirm that Wedding Outfits were added and that several ceremony choices can be customized in dialogue with a fiancé during the days before the wedding. The notes do not provide a full clothing catalogue, so this guide keeps to the categories reported by the cited sources.

## What the official 1.0 notes confirm

NPC Studio’s 1.0 patch notes say that Wedding Events and Marriage were added alongside 10-Heart Proposal Events. They also list Wedding Outfits among the new cosmetics. That establishes that wedding clothing is a real in-game cosmetic category, rather than a fan-made concept or an item from a mod.

The same notes confirm that flower color, cake flavor, and the meal served at the reception can be customized through wedding-planning dialogue with the player’s fiancé in the days leading up to the ceremony. The notes do not publish a full outfit or color list, so use the current attire screen for individual labels and variations.

## The wedding route before outfit selection

The official notes tie marriage to 10-Heart Proposal Events. They also say the Engagement Ring becomes craftable at a Forge after reaching 10 Hearts with an NPC the player is dating, with the recipe delivered by Elsie. This is the most reliable starting point for planning a wedding-focused playthrough.

A secondary guide in the collected material describes reaching relationship level 10, crafting the Engagement Ring after the Dragonsworn Tablet story quest, and proposing with it before choosing a date. That extra quest detail is not repeated in the official patch notes, so follow current story and crafting prompts for the sequence.

## What happens on the wedding day

The collected GameRant source describes Elsie and Josephine waking the player on the wedding day and bringing them to a room with wedding attire. It says the player can choose from dresses, formal suits, a wedding veil, or existing wardrobe clothing before confirming the choice and walking outside to start the ceremony. This explains why players searching for wedding outfits may not need to hunt for a single permanent clothing item first.

That description is a secondary source, so it is useful guidance rather than a replacement for an official wardrobe list. The exact interface sequence, which options appear for each save, and whether all wardrobe pieces are always available should be checked in-game. Until then, do not assume every item is unlocked, recolorable, or permanent.

## Ceremony customization and outfit planning

Plan wedding clothing together with the ceremony choices the official notes do confirm: flower color, cake flavor, and the reception meal. These are presented through dialogue with the fiancé during the lead-up to the ceremony. The notes do not state that any choice changes relationship values, gameplay rewards, or marriage eligibility.

For that reason, use the planning dialogue as a cosmetic and role-play decision unless a current official source says otherwise. If you want to preserve several possible looks, make a manual save before confirming the ceremony only if your own platform and save setup allow it. The official notes do not specify whether wedding choices can be changed after confirmation.

## Dresses, suits, veils, and existing wardrobe pieces

The available source material names dresses, formal suits, a wedding veil, and the possibility of wearing existing wardrobe clothing. It does not give an official count of dresses or suits, their names, their colors, their shops, or their prices. Those fields should not be filled with made-up values simply to make an outfit gallery look complete.

One collected secondary guide says players can choose dresses or suits regardless of body type. This is helpful for players looking for flexibility in wedding-day presentation, but the supplied official patch notes do not explicitly describe this rule. Check the current attire screen for the options available to your character.

The same caution applies to hairstyle accessories, dye options, and any claim that wedding clothes are added permanently to the wardrobe afterward. Those behaviors are not established by the cited official source, so this guide does not promise them.

## A practical checklist for wedding outfits

First, continue the romance route until the relevant 10-Heart proposal condition is available. Second, use the official wedding-planning dialogue with the fiancé to review the documented ceremony choices. Third, on the wedding day, inspect the attire selection instead of assuming an image online matches the options in your current build.

Finally, preview the final outfit against the ceremony choices before confirming. The current screen gives you a more reliable view of the available look than an older gallery.
