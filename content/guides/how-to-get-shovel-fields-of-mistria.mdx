# How to Get Shovel Fields of Mistria: Guide

For **how to get shovel Fields of Mistria**, the collected sources agree on the direct answer: buy the Worn Shovel from the General Store for 500 Tesserae. If you only see seeds or another shop category, the collected Steam discussion says to switch the General Store’s menus; controller users can use shoulder buttons, while mouse users can click the arrows. This is an early-game purchase rather than a tool handed to the player automatically.

## Buy the Worn Shovel at the General Store

[GameRant’s shovel guide](https://gamerant.com/fields-of-mistria-how-get-find-buy-shovel/) and [TheGamer’s guide](https://www.thegamer.com/fields-of-mistria-how-to-get-a-shovel-upgrade/) both identify the General Store as the place to buy a Worn Shovel. Both give its purchase price as 500 Tesserae. The [Worn Shovel Wiki entry](https://fields-of-mistria.fandom.com/wiki/Worn_Shovel) likewise says it is obtained from the General Store for 500 Tesserae and classifies it as a Tier 1 shovel.

The supplied Steam discussion addresses a common point of confusion: a player reported seeing only seeds, and replies explained that the General Store has more than one menu. The discussion says controller users can switch menus with left or right shoulder buttons, and that players using a mouse can click the menu arrows. This is community guidance, but it matches the purchase location reported by the guides.

If the item is not visible after checking the shop’s categories, do not assume it has been removed or that a quest is missing. ## What the Worn Shovel is for

The sources describe the Worn Shovel as a field-expansion and digging tool. GameRant says it can dig up grass to make more room for crops, while TheGamer says it can also carve pathways across the farm. The Wiki description adds that it is used to expand fields, excavate dig spots, and destroy crops.

That means the shovel is not only a cosmetic landscaping tool. It is also tied to finding materials from the small earthen mounds known as dig spots. GameRant names Peat and Sod as examples of materials that can come from such spots, and links this use to requests that need gardening materials.

The collected tools video makes a similar distinction: it says the shovel is used to expand the farming area and to search mounds for artifacts. It calls out a dig site west of the Narrows as a place to dig, but a specific route, yield, or guaranteed artifact from that location is **To be confirmed**. Use the in-game map and current location guides when a request asks for one particular material.

## Save for it or make the purchase first

The collected guides frame buying the Worn Shovel as the fastest route to having a shovel. GameRant suggests earning Tesserae through town requests and the Shipping Bin before making basic-tool purchases. TheGamer likewise describes buying the Worn Shovel as easier and often faster than crafting a shovel.

That is advice, not an obligation. Players may choose a different order based on their money, current requests, or crafting progress. What is confirmed by the supplied sources is that the basic tool is purchasable for 500 Tesserae and that upgraded shovels are also discussed as Blacksmith options.

Avoid interpreting “buy it first” as a claim that there is only one valid route. A Steam discussion says a shovel can also be crafted at the Blacksmith’s anvil once the player has copper. The exact prerequisites and the current crafting path must be checked against a current source before treating it as a step-by-step replacement for the store purchase.

## Shovel upgrades in the collected guides

The raw sources mention Copper, Iron, and Silver Shovels as upgrades. GameRant says there are three current shovel upgrades and names those three tiers. It also says a Copper Shovel can be purchased from the Blacksmith Shop for 1,000 Tesserae and can be charged to dig eight tiles in a row.

TheGamer presents a crafting table for the same three upgrade names: Copper Shovel with four Copper Ingots and Smithing Level 4; Iron Shovel with four Iron Ingots and Smithing Level 10; and Silver Shovel with four Silver Ingots and Smithing Level 16. It also gives a 30-minute crafting time for each in its collected text. Those are source-specific details and should be rechecked in the live game or a current official update before they are presented as permanent values.

The practical distinction is simple: the Worn Shovel gets you access to basic field work and digging, while upgrade paths are described as stronger or more efficient. Do not claim an upgrade is required for a particular quest unless a source says so. Any later tier, recipe, cost, or upgrade behavior not named here is **To be confirmed**.

## Use it on fields, crops, and dig spots

On the farm, the shovel digs grass to reveal usable ground for fields or pathways, according to the collected guides. This supports crop expansion and farm layout work. TheGamer notes that use of the shovel depletes stamina, so a player should keep stamina in mind while working.

Away from the farm, look for the small mounds or round marks described by GameRant and the tools video. The sources say these dig spots can produce materials or artifacts. They do not establish a single, universal item table for every mound, so do not promise a particular resource from an unspecified digging location.

The Worn Shovel Wiki entry also says the tool can destroy crops. That is a direct utility, but it also means a player should take care around planted areas. ## Troubleshooting the common shop-menu problem

If you have reached the General Store but only see seeds, first look for the store’s category controls. The Steam discussion specifically says to switch menus. On a controller, the referenced input is the right or left shoulder button; with a mouse, the comment says to click the arrows.

If the wrong store is being checked, return to the General Store rather than assuming the beach or another vendor sells the basic shovel. One Steam thread contains an uncertain suggestion about a beach store, but later replies in that same collected material say the General Store sells it for 500. The repeated, cross-source answer is the General Store.

When the shop still does not match the guide, record the game version and check official patch notes. The official v0.15.3 notes in the collected material mention a shovel interaction with the Magic Key in the Ruins Biome, but they do not change the sourcing advice above.
