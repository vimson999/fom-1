# Fields of Mistria Guide: A Practical First Week

This Fields of Mistria guide starts with a simple answer: plant a small first crop plot, make the Request Board part of your route, and unlock the Pickaxe early. Those actions give you a manageable routine while opening the routes to tools, resources, and town progress described in the collected guides. Do not try to finish every activity in one in-game day; the supplied video material stresses that the days are short and that focused days are normal.

## Establish a small first-day farm routine

The collected beginner material says Celine and Hayden provide a Worn Hoe and Worn Watering Can on the first day, while the player begins with a sword and trail mix. Clear a small patch, till only the tiles you plan to use, place the starter seeds, and water them each day. The same source gives spring starter examples of tulip and turnip seeds, but your own seed supply should be checked in the current game rather than assumed from an older save or video.

Starting small is practical because farming uses stamina and early inventory space is limited. One source says the character begins with ten inventory slots, so tools, forage, crops, and quest items quickly compete for room. Store items when you can and expand only at a pace that fits your available Tesserae; a collected guide reports a first bag upgrade at the General Store for 1,000 Tesserae, so check the current shop price before budgeting.

## Unlock the early tools through quests

Fields of Mistria does not hand every tool to the player at once. The supplied sources consistently describe an axe arriving through a Day Two scene with Eiland after helping with a fallen tree. They also identify the Request Board quest “Do a Bro a Favor” as the route to a Worn Pickaxe: accept it, speak with Olric, and complete the associated interaction.

The Pickaxe matters because it supports mining and later tool upgrades. Another early Request Board task, “Something’s Bugging Me,” is described as awarding a Bug Net after meeting Luc at the inn. The precise day on which either request appears can vary with progress or version, so follow the named quest route without assuming that an unavailable request has been missed permanently.

The collected sources say a Fishing Rod is sold at Terithia’s beach shop and a Shovel at the town General Store, with an older guide listing 500 Tesserae for each. They also describe an alternative: once the mines are open, copper can be gathered and used to craft copper tools without first buying the worn versions. The exact crafting requirements and current prices should be confirmed in the version you are playing, but the broader decision is sound: prioritize the tools that unlock the activity you want to do next.

## Visit the Request Board every day

The Request Board is one of the clearest sources of early direction. It is described as being near the General Store, with new requests posted regularly and no expiry once a request has been accepted. That means accepting a task does not force you to abandon farming, foraging, or exploration on the same day.

Requests can award Tesserae, food, recipes, tools, and other items according to the collected sources. They also contribute to Renown, alongside selling items and museum donations. The town’s rank rises as Renown is earned, and the rewards are collected from a chest next to the board; this makes the board a useful daily checkpoint even when you are not ready to turn in every job.

## Keep stamina in the plan

The Sleeping Dragon Inn provides a reliable early stop. Several supplied sources say the soup pot can restore 20 stamina once per day and that the soup is consumed immediately, so use it when there is room in your stamina bar. The early free bowl is consistently reported across the cited material; check the current game for later progression changes.

Juniper’s bathhouse is another option. The gathered guides describe it as restoring stamina for a Tesserae cost, and mention an early request that can reward free bath visits. The cited community and video sources do not establish a durable fee, quest date, or number of visits, so use the current prompts for those details.

Exploration can add more recovery spots. The sources describe a fountain behind a boulder on the northeastern route toward the Deep Woods, which requires a copper pickaxe to reach and restores stamina when used. A separate video mentions another fountain after the beach bridge is repaired. These are helpful landmarks, but do not build your daily route around an exact stamina total until you verify it in the live game.

## Progress through Renown, the museum, and the mines

Selling items, finishing requests, and donating discoveries to the museum are all described as Renown sources. The supplied transcript notes that the Almanac and museum indicators can help identify donation candidates and completed entries. Save one of a newly found seasonal crop or artifact when possible, because a later source says some museum collections need seasonal crops as well as finds from gathering and exploration.

The guides place the mine-opening sequence after a letter from Eiland on Spring 6 and report a Stone town rank requirement before it reopens. An older transcript gives a level value for that rank, but the current town-rank display is the safer reference for the threshold. Once open, the mines provide ore for improved tools and continue the story toward later discoveries, including magic.

## Choose one focus per day

The strongest practical lesson in the video sources is to accept that a day has limits. Pick a purpose—farming, gathering materials, requests, socializing, or a short mine run—and let the rest wait. NPC friendship is described as not decaying simply because you did not speak to someone that day, though any current relationship rule should be checked before relying on it for a challenge run.

Friday evenings at the Sleeping Dragon Inn are described as a useful social opportunity after an invitation from Reina, while the Saturday Market becomes available after repairing the bridge. The collected sources say the market stock changes and vendors offer food, recipes, hair options, clothing, and furniture. Treat shop inventories and unlock conditions as current-version details to verify, but use these recurring events as anchors for a relaxed first-week schedule.
